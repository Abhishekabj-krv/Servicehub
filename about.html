🎯 Who We Are

ServiceHub is a leading service marketplace based in Bangalore, Karnataka. We connect customers with verified, professional service providers across multiple categories including home services, wedding planning, repairs & maintenance, and professional services.

Founded with a vision to make quality services accessible to everyone, we bridge the gap between service seekers and skilled professionals. Our platform ensures reliability, transparency, and customer satisfaction at every step.
